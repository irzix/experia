# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in
the Experia community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
background or identity. We commit to acting in ways that keep the community
open, welcoming, and respectful.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies across all project spaces, including the
repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any communication carried
out on behalf of the project in public or private.

- **Scope:** project-wide

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form.
- Personal or political attacks and demeaning comments.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.

## Reporting and enforcement

Reports are handled privately by the maintainers. Because this channel is
confidential to the maintainers and does not require disclosing an incident
publicly, use GitHub's private reporting for this repository to reach them:

- **Private enforcement contact:** https://github.com/irzix/experia/security/advisories/new
- **Enforcement flow:** Reports are received privately by the maintainers, acknowledged, reviewed confidentially, and resolved with reporter anonymity preserved.

Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter
of any incident. Enforcement actions may range from a private warning to a
temporary or permanent ban from project spaces, applied consistently and
proportionately to the violation.
